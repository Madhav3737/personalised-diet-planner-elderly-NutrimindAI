# Food Nutrition and Regional Indian Cuisine Tables

## 1. Regional Indian Cuisine Food Items
The assistant must recommend foods familiar to the user's region:

- **South Indian (Tamil / Kerala / Andhra / Karnataka)**:
  - Breakfast: Idli (steamed rice cakes), Dosa (rice-lentil crepes), Upma (semolina porridge), Ragi porridge, Puttu (steamed rice and coconut).
  - Lunch/Dinner: Sambar (lentil stew), Rasam, White Rice, Brown Rice, Curd (yogurt), Cabbage Poriyal (stir-fry), Avial (mixed vegetables in coconut paste), Fish curry (Kerala).
  - Snacks: Sundal (boiled chickpeas with coconut), Banana, Roasted chana.

- **North Indian (Punjabi / UP / Delhi / Rajasthan)**:
  - Breakfast: Stuffed Roti (Paneer, Potato - dry baked), Wheat Dalia (broken wheat porridge), Poha (flattened rice), Besan Chilla (chickpea flour crepe).
  - Lunch/Dinner: Dal Tadka (yellow lentils), Dal Makhani (limit for heart patients), Roti/Phulka, Sabzi (Aloo Gobbi, Bhindi, Paneer bhurji), Curd, Khichdi.
  - Snacks: Roasted makhana (foxnuts), tea with biscuits, boiled sprout salad.

- **Bengali**:
  - Breakfast: Luchi (limit due to frying, suggest Roti), Muri (puffed rice), Oats porridge.
  - Lunch/Dinner: Macher Jhol (light fish curry), Rice, Alu Posto (potatoes in poppy seed paste), Dal, Shaak (spinach/leafy greens).

## 2. Economic Substitution and Food Costs
Food recommendations must match the user's economic status:

- **Low Income / Budget-Friendly Choices**:
  - High protein: Eggs, yellow lentils (Moong dal, Masoor dal), chickpeas, black-eyed peas, peanuts.
  - Calcium: Ragi (finger millet), local pasteurized milk, sesame seeds, seasonal green leafy vegetables (spinach, fenugreek/methi).
  - Fruits: Bananas, guavas, seasonal papayas, local oranges.
  - Whole grains: Local wheat, parboiled rice, local millets.
  - Avoid: Quinoa, avocados, blueberries, salmon, imported olive oil, chia seeds, broccoli.

- **Medium Income Choices**:
  - Grains & Protein: Whole wheat, basmati rice, Paneer (cottage cheese), curd, double-toned milk, chicken breast, local fish (Rohu, Katla).
  - Fruits: Apples, pomegranates, grapes, mangoes (seasonal).
  - Fats: Rice bran oil, mustard oil, groundnut oil.

- **High Income / Premium Choices**:
  - Grains & Protein: Quinoa, oats, almond milk, organic tofu, Greek yogurt, premium fish (Salmon, Pomfret), lean cuts.
  - Fruits: Avocados, blueberries, strawberries, kiwi, imported apples.
  - Seeds/Nuts: Almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds, pumpkin seeds.
  - Cooking fats: Extra virgin olive oil, cold-pressed avocado oil, organic A2 cow ghee.

## 3. Macro & Micronutrient Profile of Common Items (Approximate per 100g)
- **Oats**: 389 kcal, 16.9g protein, 66g carbs, 6.9g fat, 10.6g fiber. Cost: Medium.
- **Idli (2 pcs, ~100g)**: 120 kcal, 3g protein, 25g carbs, 0.5g fat, 1.5g fiber. Cost: Low.
- **Sambar (1 bowl, ~150ml)**: 90 kcal, 4g protein, 12g carbs, 3g fat, 2.5g fiber. Cost: Low.
- **Moong Dal (Cooked, 1 bowl)**: 105 kcal, 7g protein, 18g carbs, 0.5g fat, 4g fiber. Cost: Low.
- **Paneer (100g)**: 265 kcal, 18g protein, 1.2g carbs, 20g fat, 0g fiber. Cost: Medium.
- **Egg (1 whole boiled, 50g)**: 78 kcal, 6.3g protein, 0.6g carbs, 5.3g fat, 0g fiber. Cost: Low.
- **Ragi Roti (1 pc, 40g)**: 115 kcal, 2.5g protein, 24g carbs, 1g fat, 3.5g fiber. Cost: Low.
- **Wheat Roti/Phulka (1 pc, 30g)**: 85 kcal, 3g protein, 18g carbs, 0.5g fat, 2g fiber. Cost: Low.
- **Banana (1 medium)**: 89 kcal, 1.1g protein, 22.8g carbs, 0.3g fat, 2.6g fiber. Cost: Low.
- **Walnuts (28g)**: 185 kcal, 4.3g protein, 3.9g carbs, 18.5g fat, 1.9g fiber. Cost: High.
